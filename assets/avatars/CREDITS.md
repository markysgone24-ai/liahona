# Credits — Avatar Artwork

## `assets/avatars/*.svg` — Book of Mormon characters

**Source:** **Original artwork drawn from scratch for this project.**
**License:** Same license as the rest of this project.
**Author:** Created for the Liahona scripture study assistant.

These are simple original cartoon characters in a warm storybook style — round
faces, large expressive eyes, plain robes. No third-party artwork was copied,
traced, or adapted, and no existing LDS illustration was used as a source.

| File | Character | Design notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `nephi.svg` | Nephi | Young, bow and arrow |
| `lehi.svg` | Lehi | Grey hair and beard, walking staff |
| `mormon.svg` | Mormon | Headband, record book |
| `moroni.svg` | Moroni | Metal helmet, trumpet |
| `alma.svg` | Alma | Wavy hair, beard, record book |
| `ammon.svg` | Ammon | Curly hair, open smile |
| `sariah.svg` | Sariah | Veiled head covering |
| `abish.svg` | Abish | Bundle of tent cloth |
| `helaman.svg` | Helaman | Grey hair, beard, staff |
| `enos.svg` | Enos | Young, plain robe |
| `jacob.svg` | Jacob | White hair, long beard, record book |
| `ether.svg` | Ether | Wavy hair, beard, golden plates |

The characters are generic illustrations, not portraits of real people and not
depictions of the historical figures. The names refer only to the characters in
the text.
