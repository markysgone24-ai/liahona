## Brand & Style

This design system embodies contemplative calm, timeless reverence, and tactile warmth. Tailored for dedicated readers seeking focus, spiritual reflection, and distraction-free study, the experience is anchored in the physical sensibility of archival-grade printed volumes while embracing fluid modern utility.

The aesthetic philosophy draws on **Tactile Editorial Minimalist** principles:
- **Quiet Clarity:** White space functions as sacred negative space, inviting mental stillness and unhurried reading.
- **Physical Provenance:** Surfaces mimic pressed linen paper, deckled edges, and burnished gold leaf, avoiding harsh synthetic contrasts.
- **Dignified Restraint:** Decorative embellishment is reserved strictly for meaningful highlights, structural marginalia, and sacred chapter headings.

## Colors

The core palette is grounded in warm mineral and organic tones. Deep indigo-tinted navy delivers high-legibility typographic contrast without the stark clinical weight of pure black. Warm golds invoke timeless illumination, while layered warm stone tints construct physical surface hierarchies.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy distinguishes between the sacred, meditative text of the scriptures and the operational precision of reading controls.

- **Sacred Scripture & Display:** **Merriweather** provides exceptional rhythmic readability over long periods. Its sturdy serifs, generous x-height, and warm terminals render comfortably across varied pixel densities. Line heights for `scripture-verse` remain deliberately loose (1.75–1.8×) to facilitate sustained ocular tracking and annotation space.
- **Controls & Navigation:** **Plus Jakarta Sans** introduces crisp, geometric clarity to metadata, chapter references, drawer navigation, and verse numbers.
- **Verse Numerals:** Superscript verse markers are typeset in `Plus Jakarta Sans` Bold at 11px, tinted in muted sacred gold (`#C9A227`), keeping them instantly discoverable without interrupting continuous reading cadence.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout geometry honors classical book proportions. Vertical rhythm centers on an 8pt base grid with a 4pt subgrid for fine typography and badge alignment.

- **Mobile Reading View:** Single fluid column bound by `margin` (20px). Body reading width is strictly constrained to a maximum of 68 characters per line (`max-width: 640px`) centered dynamically within wider viewports to preserve scan lines.
- **Split-Screen & Marginalia (Tablet/Desktop):** At tablet breakpoints (`>= 768px`), layout expands into an asymmetric dual-column structure: primary scripture text occupies 65% of the viewport width, while cross-references, study notes, and commentary stream within a 35% margin rail separated by an organic 1px vertical hairline rule.
- **Bottom Clearance:** Reading views maintain dynamic bottom padding (`space-3xl`) to prevent content obstruction from floating reading toolbars and thumb-zone controls.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses surface color layering paired with warm ambient shadows, avoiding hard geometric drop shadows.

- **Atmospheric Tinting:** Shadow dropouts are never pure black; they are cast with 6% to 12% opacity warm slate (`#1B2A41`) in Day Mode and 30% opacity midnight abyss (`#050A10`) in Night Mode.
- **Elevation Layers:**
  - **Level 0 (Parchment Foundation):** Flat background, un-elevated.
  - **Level 1 (Study Cards & Verses):** Surface `#F3ECE0`, subtle border `#E5DBCB`, shadow `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(27, 42, 65, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(27, 42, 65, 0.03)`.
  - **Level 2 (Floating Action Toolbar & Menus):** Surface `#FAF6EE`, shadow `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(27, 42, 65, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(27, 42, 65, 0.04)`.
  - **Level 3 (Modal Sheets & Footnotes):** Surface `#FAF6EE`, border-t `1px solid #E5DBCB`, shadow `0 -12px 32px -4px rgba(27, 42, 65, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The shape system balances modern mobile ergonomics with the soft, worn corners of handcrafted leather-bound texts.

- **Cards & Sheets:** Base curvature is calibrated to 16px (`rounded-lg`) and 20px (`rounded-xl`), creating comfortable touch surfaces on handheld devices.
- **Tags & Floating Pills:** Pill profiles (`rounded-full`) are reserved for interactive chapter selectors, verse selection toggles, and context tags.
- **Decorative Accent Rules:** Section divider marks employ tapered ends and thin 1px horizontal hairlines reminiscent of lithographic press marks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Sacred Action:** Deep Navy background (`#1B2A41`), soft cream text (`#FAF6EE`), `rounded-lg` (16px), height 48px, horizontal padding `space-lg`. On press, dims to 92% opacity with micro-scale (0.98).
- **Secondary / Study Action:** Background transparent, 1px solid border (`#E5DBCB`), text `#1B2A41`. Active state shifts surface to `#EAE2D2`.
- **Illuminated / Gold Action:** Background `#C9A227`, text `#FAF6EE`, subtle golden glow shadow `0 4px 14px rgba(201, 162, 39, 0.25)`.

### Cards (Study Journal & Reading Plans)
- Built on `#F3ECE0` with 1px border `#E5DBCB` and `rounded-xl` (20px). Inner padding set to `space-lg`.
- Top edge incorporates an optional 2px accent strip in Gold (`#C9A227`) for marked chapters or daily study targets.

### Scripture Chips & Tag Filters
- Height 32px, `rounded-full`, background `#EAE2D2`, text `#1B2A41` using `label-md`.
- Selected state switches background to `#1B2A41` with `#FAF6EE` typography, or `#C9A227` with white typography for active scripture cross-reference filters.

### Lists & Chapter Navigator
- Flat dividers in `#E5DBCB` with 0px margin bleed.
- Navigator cells feature left-aligned chapter numerals in `headline-sm`, trailing label for reading duration in `marginalia` (`#5C6B73`), and comfortable 56px touch target heights.

### Input Fields & Reflection Journal
- Inset background `#FAF6EE` set within container `#F3ECE0`. 1px border `#E5DBCB`, transitioning to `#C9A227` upon focus with 2px inner ring.
- Placeholder text in `#8C7051` (warm umber tint) using `body-md`.

### Scripture Verse Highlight & Interaction
- **Selection Highlight:** Soft luminous yellow tint (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.22)`) with rounded inline box decoration (3px radius).
- **Floating Reading Palette:** Compact pill toolbar housing highlight colors, tag actions, personal note creation, and text-to-speech audio playback. Surface styled with Level 2 elevation.
